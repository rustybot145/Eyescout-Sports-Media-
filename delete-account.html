Delete your EyeScout account

Google Play requires that account deletion can be requested from the web, not only inside the app. Both routes do exactly the same thing.

Fastest: delete it in the app

Settings → Account → Delete Account. It takes effect immediately and needs no approval from us.

Or request it here

Email info@eyescoutsports.com from the address on your EyeScout account, with the subject Delete my account. If you cannot email from that address, tell us the account's email and full name so we can verify it is yours.

We action verified requests within 7 days. If a parent or guardian is requesting deletion of a minor's account, say so — we do not require the athlete's own confirmation.

What gets deleted

Permanently, and it cannot be undone:

  • Your profile and every field on it, including the parent/guardian contact
  • Every post you made, and all photos and videos you uploaded — the files are removed from storage, not just unlinked
  • Your hypes, follows, saved players, blocks, and reports you filed
  • Your messages
  • Your subscription record, and your login itself

What we keep, and for how long

Records connected to a child-safety report are retained for as long as the law requires (18 U.S.C. §2258A), separately from your account. Google Play holds its own billing records under its policy — deleting your EyeScout account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that in Play first.
