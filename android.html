Test EyeScout on Android

Two quick steps. Both are needed — please don't skip the first.

1
Join the testers group

This is what gives your Google account access. Use the same Google account your phone's Play Store is signed in to.

Join the group
2
Become a tester, then install

Open this and tap “Become a tester”. The download link appears on that page once you do.

Get the app
If step 2 says the app can't be found — that's normal right after joining. Google can take a few hours to register new group members. Give it some time and open the link again; nothing is broken and you don't need to sign up a second time.

Good to know

  • The app is completely free. No subscription, no in-app purchases.
  • It's for athletes 13 and up. Signup asks for a parent or guardian's contact details so account and safety notices reach an adult.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
