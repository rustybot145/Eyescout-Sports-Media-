The Story

Built From
Love of the Game

"My name is Xavier Perez and I started Eyescout Sports because I love sports — always have. Baseball, football, volleyball, soccer — I played it, watched it, and lived it."

Started out in tech — telecom, SIP and PRI voice services. Built a career, stayed connected to the sports world the whole time.

Saw the gap: parents spending everything on travel sports, private coaches, and tournaments — then handing college coaches iPhone footage. Athletes with real talent getting passed over because they lacked exposure.

Pivoted from tech into sports media in 2025. Launched Eyescout Sports in Phoenix, AZ. Partnered with the Arizona Youth Football League. 92+ sessions and counting.

Now on the sidelines, behind the lens — game days, tournaments, cheer competitions, Friday night lights. Making sure every moment gets captured and every athlete gets seen.

"Film Em. Scout Em. Stardom. That's not just a tagline. That's the mission."
