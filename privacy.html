Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy explains what Eyescout Sports collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers our website at eyescoutsports.com and the EyeScout Sports mobile apps for iOS and Android.

Short version. We collect the account details you give us, the photos and videos you choose to post, and basic usage data needed to run the service. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Eyescout Sports ("we", "us") provides sports photography, videography, and a platform where athletes build a sports resume and coaches discover talent. For any privacy question, contact info@eyescoutsports.com.

Payments

We never see or store your full card number. Mobile subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google through in-app purchase. Website purchases are processed by PayPal. Those companies handle your payment details under their own privacy policies.

3. How we use it

  • To run your profile, feed, messages, and subscription
  • To deliver photos and videos you have ordered
  • To let verified coaches discover athletes — the core purpose of the platform
  • To send service messages: confirmations, photo-ready notices, password resets, and activity notifications (a new message, a coach following you). You can turn activity notification emails off in Settings
  • To keep the platform safe: reviewing reported content, enforcing our rules
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not run third-party ad trackers, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

4. Who can see your content

  • Your profile and posts are visible to signed-in athletes and to verified coaches on the platform. Assume anything you post can be seen by any coach.
  • Shared links. If you share a post, anyone holding that link can view that single photo or video — including people without an account. They cannot see the rest of your profile, your other posts, or your messages. A share link stays valid until the post is deleted.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you are messaging. Staff may access messages only to investigate a report or a safety concern.

5. Athletes under 18, and under 13

Parents and guardians: if your child is under 13, an account must be created and managed by you, using your email as the contact address. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

Much of our community is school-age. We ask for a parent or guardian contact so that consent, bookings, photo delivery, and account notices go to an adult. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without parental consent, email info@eyescoutsports.com and we will delete the account and its content promptly.

Parents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's information at any time by emailing us from the parent contact address on file.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, and messages are deleted. Some records — order and payment history — are retained where we are legally required to keep them. Backups roll off within 30 days. Content you shared via a public link becomes inaccessible once the post is deleted.

8. Your choices

  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account, in the app. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Edit your information — any time from your profile.
  • Turn off notification emails — Settings.
  • Manage your subscription — through your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Access or export your data — email us and we will provide a copy.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection). Email info@eyescoutsports.com and we will honour them. We will not treat you differently for exercising a privacy right.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it. Database access rules restrict what any signed-in account can read. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you as required by law.

10. Where data is stored

Our providers operate in the United States. If you use EyeScout from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed there.

11. Changes

If we make a material change we will update the date above and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app or by email.
