EyeScout Sports — Child Safety Standards

1. Our prohibition on CSAE

EyeScout Sports strictly prohibits, on any part of the service:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Sexualisation of a minor, including suggestive framing, posing, or commentary about a minor's body.
  • Grooming — building a relationship with a minor to facilitate sexual contact, online or offline.
  • Sextortion, or soliciting sexual imagery from a minor.
  • Trafficking of a minor, or any offer to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Off-platform solicitation of minors initiated through EyeScout.

Violation results in immediate account termination, preservation of the account record, and reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement where required.

2. How users report

Every post carries an options control that opens Report post with the following categories, including Nudity or sexual content and Harassment or bullying:

It's spam · Nudity or sexual content · Harassment or bullying · Violence or dangerous content · Hate speech or symbols · False information · Scam or fraud · Something else

Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed by EyeScout staff. The same control offers Block, which immediately hides that user's content from the reporter and the reporter's content from them.

Reports involving a minor are prioritised ahead of all other queue items.

3. How we respond

Step Action Target
1 Report received, content flagged for review Immediate
2 Suspected CSAE content removed pending review Within 24 hours
3 Account suspended if violation confirmed Within 24 hours
4 NCMEC CyberTipline report filed for apparent CSAM Without delay, as required by 18 U.S.C. §2258A
5 Reporter notified of outcome Within 7 days

4. Account and age requirements

  • Minimum age 13. Age is confirmed at signup before an account can be created.
  • Accounts for athletes collect a parent/guardian name, email, and phone so a guardian can be reached about their child's account.
  • Coach accounts are reviewed and approved before gaining access to athlete profiles or messaging.
  • Athlete contact details are never exposed to coaches through the app. The parent/guardian name, email, and phone are held for our own contact with a guardian and are not visible to coaches or other users.

5. Compliance with law

EyeScout complies with applicable child-safety law, including COPPA and 18 U.S.C. §2258A reporting obligations. We preserve records associated with reported CSAE for the period required and respond to valid legal process from law enforcement.

6. Child safety point of contact

info@eyescoutsports.com

This address is monitored and is the designated contact for Google Play and for law enforcement inquiries relating to child safety.

7. Review

These standards are reviewed at least annually and after any material change to the service.
