Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, and messages, and cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account — deleting the account does not cancel store billing.

How do I cancel EyeScout Pro?

Pro is billed by Apple or Google, so cancel it there:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → EyeScout Sports → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → EyeScout Sports → Cancel

You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

I paid for Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open the paywall screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the store receipt.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap the ··· menu on any post. You can report the post or block the person who posted it. Reports are reviewed and content that breaks our rules is removed. For anything urgent involving a minor's safety, email us directly and we will act immediately.

When will my photos be ready?

You will get an email as soon as your gallery is ready to view and select from. If it has been longer than expected, email us with the date and location of your session.

Can a parent manage the account?

Yes — and for athletes under 13 a parent or guardian must. Add the parent's email as the contact on the account so all notices go to an adult. Parents can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's information by emailing us from that address.

Password reset isn't arriving

Check spam, then confirm you are using the email the account was created with. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, email us and we will reset it manually.

Safety

This platform is used by minors. Content that sexualises a minor, or any adult attempting inappropriate contact with a minor, results in immediate permanent removal and referral to law enforcement. Report it in-app, or email info@eyescoutsports.com with "SAFETY" in the subject line and we will prioritise it.
