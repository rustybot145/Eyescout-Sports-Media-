How the player photo portal works — Xavier's quick guide

The Big Picture

What this system does

After a tournament, players log into the portal, browse the photos you shot, pick their favorites, and submit. You get the list of what each player wants, edit those shots, and we deliver them back. That's the whole loop.

Two types of players

Purchased already paid — they get their edited photos delivered.

Not Purchased haven't paid yet — they can still browse and pick favorites, but they have to purchase before they get the final edits.

Your Workflow — After Every Tournament

1

Upload all photos to Dropbox

Drop the full tournament dump into a Dropbox folder. This is the folder players will browse to find their shots.

2

Add the tournament in admin

Go to the admin panel → Tournaments → Create Tournament. Fill in the name, date, sport, and paste the Dropbox folder link. This makes the tournament appear in the player portal.

3

Players log in and pick their photos

Players go to the portal, log in, select their tournament, click the Dropbox link to browse, then mark which photos they want. They hit submit when done.

4

You see their selections in admin

Open the Players tab in admin. Any player with Needs Photos has submitted their picks. Open their profile to see exactly which shots they selected.

5

Edit and deliver

Edit the selected photos. Create a new Dropbox folder with just their finished shots. Go into the player's profile in admin, paste the delivery link, and flip "Photos Ready." They'll see their photos when they log back in.

Admin Panel — What's Where

Dashboard

Quick overview — total players, who's purchased, who's waiting on photos, recent signups.

Players

Full list of everyone who signed up. Use the Filter button to sort by purchased, not purchased, sport, or tournament. Click any player to open their profile — you'll see their contact info, selected photos, and delivery status.

Tournaments

Create and manage tournaments here. Each tournament has a Dropbox link field — that's the folder players click to browse photos. Click "Manage →" on a tournament to see its details and the purchased players assigned to it.

Purchase Codes

Generate a code and give it to a paying customer. When they sign up and enter the code, they're automatically marked as purchased — no manual step needed.

Quick Notes

Dropbox links: When you paste a Dropbox folder link, the portal automatically pulls every photo from that folder and displays them right inside the app. Players browse and pick their shots inline — they never leave the portal or open Dropbox.
Delivery link: When you're done editing, create a fresh Dropbox folder with only the finished shots and paste that link into the player's profile in admin. Keep the raw and edited folders separate.
Non-purchased players: Let them pick favorites — you don't edit their photos until they pay. Their selections are saved so once they purchase, you already know what they want.
